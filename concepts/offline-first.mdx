title: Offline-First Architecture
description: How Wede ensures operational continuity without internet
---

## The Problem

Mission-critical operations cannot stop when internet connectivity fails.

## The Solution

Wede uses a cascading fallback chain:

- Internet (REST) — primary channel
- SMS — first fallback
- Voice — second fallback
- Offline Token — last resort

## Connectivity States

- **online** — Full internet connectivity
- **degraded** — Intermittent connectivity, fallback active
- **sms\_only** — Internet unavailable, SMS operational
- **offline** — All channels down, offline tokens active
